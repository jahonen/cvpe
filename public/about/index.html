About

CVPE — Certa Vizio Pri Eŭropo, "a certain vision of Europe," in Esperanto: a language invented by a Polish oculist precisely because no nation owned it. The site publishes essays on European sovereignty, security doctrine, institutional architecture, and civic identity; a lexicon of coined terms; and an accusation audit of where Europe falls short of its own stated values.

The house discipline is Ahonen's Razor: problem, attribution, and solution kept strictly separate. Predictions are logged with timestamps and revisited in public, whether they aged well or not.

The author

JP Ahonen is a Finnish-Greek transformation director, strategic advisor, and senior non-commissioned officer in the Finnish defence reserves (Ylivääpeli, OR-8; 26 years of service). Professional background: enterprise cloud transformation, EMEA public sector advisory, and founding companies. He writes from Porto Rafti, Greece, and Helsinki, Finland.

Contact and discussion: @jpahonen.eurosky.social on Bluesky.
