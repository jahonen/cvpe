A Certain Vision · Star Three

Civic Belonging as Practice

Rigel — foot of the hunter

Being part of Europe is something you do, not just something you are.

Belonging is built through the time and effort people put in: showing up, contributing, taking part in the life of the place they live. It isn't only about where someone was born, or what passport they were handed at birth.

This doesn't erase where people come from. It just means belonging has to be lived, actively, and not only claimed by birthright.
